# Shreeya's script: opening, demo, impact to close

About 3 min 15 s of talking plus the 41 s video, so about 4 minutes in all. Bold words are the ones
to stress. Slide numbers are the corner labels in the deck.

## 1. Title (~8 s)

"Good morning. We're Team DermEdge from IIIT Sri City. DermEdge is **private AI eczema screening and
flare tracking**, on your own phone."

## 2. Problem (~35 s)

"Eczema has the **highest burden of any skin disease**: about **204 million** people live with it,
and **8.5% of school children** in Central India. But a dermatologist sees **one snapshot every few
weeks**. The flares happen in between, and **nothing records them**.

Two more problems. Psoriasis, fungal infection and scabies **look like eczema** but need different
treatment. And most skin AI is trained on **lighter skin**: dermatology atlases hold **3.6 times**
more light-skin images than dark."

## 3. Our solution (~25 s)

"DermEdge is a **camera check, a wearable band and a doctor report**, all on your own device. One
photo gives **three answers**: is it a rash, is it eczema, how severe is it. The band records
scratching, heart rate and skin temperature. And it's **built and tested for South Asian skin**."

## 4. Why DermEdge is different (~35 s)

"So what's actually different?
**Today**, nothing records the weeks between visits. **DermEdge** puts photo history and band data in
**one doctor report**.
**Today**, most skin AI misses brown skin. We catch **84%** of rashes on South Asian hospital photos,
**up from 33%**.
**Today**, look-alikes get called eczema. We **flag them** and say see a doctor.
And **today**, skin apps upload your photos. Ours runs **offline, on your phone**. No photo ever
leaves it.
Let us show you."

## 5. Demo video (41 s, press play)

Short cues over the silent video, or let it run:
- Phone opens: "Installed straight from the browser, **no app store**."
- Healthy skin: "Healthy skin: **no rash**."
- Rash: "A rash, captured **automatically**: eczema, moderate. All **on the phone**."
- Laptop: "Eczema on **darker skin**, caught. Fungal infection and psoriasis: **flagged as
  look-alikes**, see a doctor."

Hand over to the next speaker for slides 6-13.

## 14. Impact (~40 s; slow down, this is what the judges score)

"Here's why this matters. **204 million** people. A check that's **free** on any phone browser, with
no servers, and it **works offline**, so it reaches places with bad internet. And **84%** of rashes
caught on South Asian skin.
For **patients**: a private check in seconds, at home. For **families**: knowing when to see a
doctor. For **doctors**: the weeks between visits, in one report. And where there are **few
dermatologists**: a free first check.
It's a **screening aid, not a diagnosis**. It gets people to the doctor sooner, with better data.
**Care that doesn't stop between visits.**"

## 15. What dermatologists told us (~20 s)

"We spoke to practising dermatologists. **Every one of them** checks how **dry** and how **warm** the
skin is around the rash, but **only by hand, at the visit**. Research backs this up: in the
**Southampton** study, eczema skin read drier in **13 of 13** patients."

## 16. Future scope (~25 s)

"So next, we **measure more**: a soft skin patch on the rash for moisture and temperature. Then
**smarter models**, from the data the band collects. And we **reach more people**: a lighter model
for low-end phones, and clinic partnerships.
DermEdge brings **private, fair eczema care home**: a check in seconds, tracking in between, and
data your doctor can act on."

## Thank you (~7 s)

"Thank you. **Scan the QR** to try DermEdge yourself. We're happy to take questions."

## If time runs short

Cut in this order: the 3.6× atlas line (slide 2), the band sentence (slide 3), the Southampton
sentence (slide 15). Never cut slide 4 or Impact.

## Don't say

"Diagnose" (say "check" or "screen"), "the best" (say "built and tested for South Asian skin"),
"we trained a model" for Southampton, or any stress accuracy.
