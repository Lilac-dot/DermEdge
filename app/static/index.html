Does it work across skin tones?

Stage 1 (normal vs rash) on held-out real smartphone photos, by Fitzpatrick skin type. At one shared threshold the model missed more rashes on darker skin, even though it ranked them almost as well (similar AUC). Choosing your skin type applies a calibrated threshold.

Skin type V–VI has only 16 healthy and 114 rash photos, and its threshold was tuned on them, so its calibrated numbers are a demonstrated fix, not a validated one.
